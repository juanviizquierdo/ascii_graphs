# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints, and
focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
intimidation, publication of private information, or sustained disruption.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants who violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be
handled confidentially and reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible.
